## M26-A-CLOZE-01 · cloze · 健康心理

词数约 266

Most students meet real pressure long before their final year. A teenager who feels (1) ______ the night before an important exam is not weak, and the feeling is a normal signal that something matters. What separates those who cope from those who do not is rarely talent; it is usually a small (2) ______ of habits, repeated over months.

One useful habit is to (3) ______ a large task down into smaller parts. A student who can do this stops feeling buried under the whole thing. Another habit is to (4) ______ how much time each step really needs, instead of guessing. Adults often (5) ______ young people to talk about their worries, yet many teenagers stay (6) ______ about them, because they fear looking foolish.

Sleep matters more than most revision plans. A body that is (7) ______ cannot hold what it learned the day before, and memory suffers first. Some students try to (8) ______ the problem with strong coffee, which helps for an hour and then makes everything worse.

Over a term, small choices (9) ______ change the picture. Nobody becomes (10) ______ in a week, but almost anyone can become steadier. When a plan fails, the sensible response is to (11) ______ rather than to blame yourself. Friends help most when they listen without judging, and a teacher who notices a quiet student can (12) ______ that student to ask for help. In the end, (13) ______ with a slow process matters more than any single result. The (14) ______ of a difficult month is often a (15) ______ person, not a weaker one. None of this happens overnight, and no single trick replaces steady effort.

**1. Choose the best option for blank 1.**

- A. anxious
- B. cheerful
- C. eager
- D. calm

答案：anxious

解析：句意为“大考前一夜感到焦虑的学生并不软弱，这种感觉说明这件事对他很重要”，anxious 表示担忧、紧张，与后句“是一种正常信号”呼应。cheerful 与 eager 是积极情绪，后面不会引出“需要安静下来”的语境；calm 与焦虑相反，且与“不是软弱”这一让步逻辑冲突。本题考点是情绪形容词与语境的匹配。

**2. Choose the best option for blank 2.**

- A. number
- B. amount
- C. deal
- D. quantity

答案：number

解析：habits 是可数名词，应用 a small number of；a small amount of 与 quantity of 只接不可数名词，a deal of 不能单独使用（只有 a great deal of）。本题考点是可数、不可数搭配。

**3. Choose the best option for blank 3.**

- A. break
- B. cut
- C. divide
- D. separate

答案：break

解析：break ... down into 意为“把……拆解成”，与空后的 down into smaller parts 构成固定搭配。divide 与 separate 虽可表示“分开”，但不能与 down 连用；cut down 意为“削减”，不能接 into。本题考点是短语动词搭配。

**4. Choose the best option for blank 4.**

- A. estimate
- B. measure
- C. count
- D. record

答案：estimate

解析：句意为“估出每一步实际需要多少时间”，estimate 用于对尚未发生的事做预估；measure、count、record 都针对已经存在的对象，与 instead of guessing（而不是瞎猜）指向的“预测”不符。本题考点是近义动词的时间指向。

**5. Choose the best option for blank 5.**

- A. encourage
- B. allow
- C. order
- D. force

答案：encourage

解析：句意为“成年人常常鼓励年轻人谈自己的烦恼”，encourage sb to do 表示主动引导。allow 只表许可，不含引导意味；order 与 force 带强制色彩，与“谈烦恼”这种需要信任的场合不符。本题考点是动词的语气强度。

**6. Choose the best option for blank 6.**

- A. silent
- B. honest
- C. open
- D. calm

答案：silent

解析：because they fear looking foolish 说明他们选择不说，因此是 stay silent about their worries。honest 与 open 都与“怕显得愚蠢”直接矛盾；calm 指情绪状态，不能与 about their worries 搭配表示“闭口不谈”。本题考点是形容词与介词短语的搭配。

**7. Choose the best option for blank 7.**

- A. exhausted
- B. hungry
- C. restless
- D. injured

答案：exhausted

解析：后文 memory suffers first 与“无法保存前一天所学”指向睡眠不足导致的身体疲惫，exhausted 最贴切。hungry 与记忆无关；restless 指坐立不安，是结果而非原因；injured 在文中没有任何依据。本题考点是根据因果线索选择形容词。

**8. Choose the best option for blank 8.**

- A. escape
- B. solve
- C. explain
- D. remove

答案：escape

解析：后文 which helps for an hour and then makes everything worse 说明咖啡只是暂时回避问题，因此是 escape the problem。solve 与 remove 都表示真正解决，与“一小时后更糟”矛盾；explain 不能与 the problem with coffee 搭配表达这一含义。本题考点是动词与后文评价的呼应。

**9. Choose the best option for blank 9.**

- A. gradually
- B. instantly
- C. occasionally
- D. merely

答案：gradually

解析：本段主旨是“一个学期里的小选择慢慢改变局面”，gradually 表示渐进，与 over a term 和时间积累一致。instantly 与之相反；occasionally 表频率而非变化方式；merely 意为“仅仅”，不含过程含义。本题考点是副词的方式义。

**10. Choose the best option for blank 10.**

- A. confident
- B. perfect
- C. famous
- D. wealthy

答案：confident

解析：空后 but almost anyone can become steadier 构成对照，与“稳”并列的是“自信”，因此选 confident。perfect 过于绝对，任何人都无法在一周内做到；famous 与 wealthy 与“学业压力与自我管理”的主题无关。本题考点是并列结构中的语义对应。

**11. Choose the best option for blank 11.**

- A. adjust
- B. apologize
- C. complain
- D. quit

答案：adjust

解析：rather than to blame yourself 要求前面是一个积极的应对方式，adjust 表示调整做法。apologize 是道歉，与“计划失败”无直接关系；complain 与 blame 同属消极反应，不能构成对比；quit 是放弃，与全文“坚持下去”的主旨相反。本题考点是转折结构中的态度判断。

**12. Choose the best option for blank 12.**

- A. persuade
- B. permit
- C. expect
- D. force

答案：persuade

解析：老师注意到沉默的学生后“说服他去求助”，persuade sb to do 表示劝服。permit 是允许，学生本来就可以求助，不存在许可问题；expect 只表期待，不含说服这一动作；force 是强迫，与 can 的温和语气不符。本题考点是动词的人际含义。

**13. Choose the best option for blank 13.**

- A. patience
- B. courage
- C. energy
- D. power

答案：patience

解析：patience with a slow process 是地道搭配，意为“对缓慢过程的耐心”，与全文“成长需要时间”一致。courage 与 energy 不能接 with a process；power with 也不成立。本题考点是名词与介词的固定搭配。

**14. Choose the best option for blank 14.**

- A. reward
- B. cost
- C. price
- D. burden

答案：reward

解析：空后是 a stronger person，属于收获，因此用 the reward of a difficult month。cost、price、burden 都表示代价或负担，与“变得更坚强”这一正面结果方向相反。本题考点是名词的褒贬方向。

**15. Choose the best option for blank 15.**

- A. stronger
- B. calmer
- C. wiser
- D. kinder

答案：stronger

解析：空后 not a weaker one 构成比较级对照，与 weaker 直接对应的是 stronger。calmer、wiser、kinder 本身都成立，但都不能与 weaker 构成同一维度的反义对照，因此不是最佳答案。本题考点是比较级在对照结构中的呼应。

## M26-A-CLOZE-02 · cloze · 职业创业

词数约 272

When Lin Wei opened a small repair shop at twenty-three, he believed that skill alone would bring customers. Six months later he was still (1) ______ money, and he could not explain why. His father, who had run a grocery for thirty years, told him that a shop lives on trust rather than on (2) ______.

The first habit Lin had to learn was to (3) ______ the costs honestly. He had (4) ______ the price of parts and forgotten the rent, the electricity and his own time. The second was to (5) ______ what customers actually wanted, instead of what he himself liked. An old man once brought in a radio that was forty years old. Lin tried to (6) ______ him to buy a new one, but the man asked only for a battery.

Slowly Lin learned to (7) ______ his money. He set a fixed (8) ______ aside every week for parts, and he stopped (9) ______ tools that he would never use. He also learned to (10) ______ with suppliers instead of arguing; a calm phone call usually brought a better price than a long complaint.

The shop did not grow (11) ______. In the first year he gained perhaps twenty regular customers, and most came back because he (12) ______ the job properly. A neighbour who grew flowers told him that a good name is built in small pieces and destroyed in one afternoon.

What finally changed was not a new machine but a notebook. Every evening Lin wrote down one thing he had done badly and one thing he would (13) ______ differently. That habit cost nothing, yet in two years it (14) ______ his whole way of working. He now says that the greatest (15) ______ a young trader can have is not capital but the willingness to be corrected.

**1. Choose the best option for blank 1.**

- A. losing
- B. wasting
- C. spending
- D. borrowing

答案：losing

解析：lose money 意为“亏钱”，与 and he could not explain why 呼应，说明店铺在亏损。spending money 是经营中的正常行为，不能构成“说不清为什么”的困惑；wasting money 需要“浪费”的具体证据，文中未给；borrowing 是借钱，与空后“无法解释原因”不衔接。本题考点是动词与 money 的搭配义。

**2. Choose the best option for blank 2.**

- A. speed
- B. price
- C. size
- D. luck

答案：speed

解析：lives on trust rather than on ______ 是正反对比结构，与 trust 相对的是 speed（“靠信任而不是靠速度”）。price 与 size 都属于经营要素，不能与 trust 形成对立；luck 与“信用”不是同一维度。本题考点是转折对比中的语义对举。

**3. Choose the best option for blank 3.**

- A. calculate
- B. reduce
- C. ignore
- D. copy

答案：calculate

解析：calculate the costs honestly 意为“如实核算成本”，与后文“漏算房租、电费”形成因果关系。reduce 与 honestly 语义不合（省钱与诚实不是一回事）；ignore 恰是后文批判的行为，此处不能选；copy 与成本无关。本题考点是动词与副词的语义匹配。

**4. Choose the best option for blank 4.**

- A. underestimated
- B. accepted
- C. compared
- D. recorded

答案：underestimated

解析：后文 and forgotten the rent, the electricity and his own time 说明他低估了成本，underestimate 表示“估低”，与“亏钱”直接因果。accept 表示接受报价，不会导致亏损；compare 与 record 都不产生“漏算”的后果，record 更与“忘记”矛盾。本题考点是动词与后果的逻辑关系。

**5. Choose the best option for blank 5.**

- A. discover
- B. decide
- C. repeat
- D. hide

答案：discover

解析：discover what customers actually wanted 意为“弄清顾客真正想要什么”，与 instead of what he himself liked 形成对照。decide 的主语应是人做选择，不能是“顾客想要什么”；repeat 与 hide 都不能与 what 从句构成“了解需求”的含义。本题考点是动词与宾语从句的搭配。

**6. Choose the best option for blank 6.**

- A. persuade
- B. allow
- C. order
- D. remind

答案：persuade

解析：Lin tried to persuade him to buy a new one 意为“试图说服他买新的”，与 but the man asked only for a battery 构成转折。allow 是许可，顾客买什么是自己的决定；order 用于上级对下级；remind 是提醒，与“推销新机”不符。本题考点是动词的交际功能。

**7. Choose the best option for blank 7.**

- A. manage
- B. waste
- C. borrow
- D. hide

答案：manage

解析：manage his money 意为“打理钱”，与后文“每周固定留出一笔钱、不再乱买工具”一致。waste、borrow、hide 都是负面或无关行为，与“学会”这一正面变化矛盾。本题考点是动词与下文的概括关系。

**8. Choose the best option for blank 8.**

- A. sum
- B. level
- C. rate
- D. score

答案：sum

解析：set a fixed sum aside 意为“固定留出一笔钱”，sum 指金额。level 指水平，rate 指比率，score 指分数，都不能与 set aside（留出）搭配表示一笔钱。本题考点是名词与量语义的搭配。

**9. Choose the best option for blank 9.**

- A. buying
- B. selling
- C. repairing
- D. lending

答案：buying

解析：stopped buying tools that he would never use 意为“不再买用不上的工具”，与“省钱”一致。selling 与“用不上”逻辑冲突（卖掉的正是用不上的）；repairing 与 lending 都不产生“闲置”的结果。本题考点是动名词与后置定语的关系。

**10. Choose the best option for blank 10.**

- A. negotiate
- B. argue
- C. meet
- D. compete

答案：negotiate

解析：negotiate with suppliers 意为“与供应商谈价”，与 instead of arguing 和“平静的电话能拿到更好的价格”呼应。argue 与 instead of arguing 重复且被明确排除；meet with 只表见面，不含议价；compete with 是竞争，与“合作拿价”相反。本题考点是动词与后文评价的对应。

**11. Choose the best option for blank 11.**

- A. rapidly
- B. safely
- C. quietly
- D. equally

答案：rapidly

解析：The shop did not grow rapidly 意为“店铺没有快速扩张”，与后文“第一年只多了二十个老客户”呼应。safely、quietly、equally 都不是描述增长速度的词，与 grow 搭配后语义不成立。本题考点是副词与动词的搭配义。

**12. Choose the best option for blank 12.**

- A. completed
- B. refused
- C. delayed
- D. copied

答案：completed

解析：most came back because he completed the job properly 意为“因为他把活干好了才回头”，completed 与 properly 搭配表示“做得妥当”。refused 与 delayed 都会让顾客不再上门，与 came back 矛盾；copied 与修理工的工作不符。本题考点是因果句中的动词选择。

**13. Choose the best option for blank 13.**

- A. handle
- B. forget
- C. avoid
- D. repeat

答案：handle

解析：one thing he would handle differently 意为“下次会换个方式处理的一件事”，handle 与 differently 搭配自然。forget 与 avoid 后接 differently 语义不通；repeat differently 自相矛盾。本题考点是动词与方式状语的搭配。

**14. Choose the best option for blank 14.**

- A. reshaped
- B. delayed
- C. copied
- D. reduced

答案：reshaped

解析：in two years it reshaped his whole way of working 意为“两年里重塑了他的整个工作方式”，与“记笔记这一习惯”带来的改变呼应。delayed 与 reduced 都表示减少或推迟，与“整个方式改变”不符；copied 语义不通。本题考点是动词与宾语的程度匹配。

**15. Choose the best option for blank 15.**

- A. advantage
- B. risk
- C. cost
- D. duty

答案：advantage

解析：the greatest advantage ... is not capital but the willingness to be corrected 意为“最大的优势不是本钱，而是愿意被纠正”，advantage 与 not ... but ... 的褒义结构一致。risk 与 cost 是负面项，duty 是义务，都不能与“愿意被纠正”并列。本题考点是名词的褒贬与对比结构。

## M26-A-GRAMMAR-01 · grammar_fill · 家庭代际

词数约 210

In many families, the older and the younger generation share one roof, and small duties often create quiet tension between them. Last winter, a volunteer (1) ______ at the front door turned out to be a neighbour who had come to ask about the family's holiday plan. The grandfather often points at the bridge, (2) ______ in 1980 and still carrying traffic, and he likes to tell the children how the town has changed. The mother keeps a notebook in which she (3) ______ the small events of each day, and she reads it aloud when the family meets for dinner. (4) ______ the plan carefully with her husband, she decided to give the grandparents the larger room. The children were not sure (5) ______, so they asked many questions during the meal. (6) ______ the weather turned cold, the family still walked to the park on Sunday. The father often says that, (7) ______ in charge, he would ask every member to share one household task each week. Since the new arrangement began, most of the shopping (8) ______ by the grandparents. On school days, (9) ______ leaves the station at seven, so the student must finish breakfast early. Such small habits, (10) ______ patiently over years, hold a family together.

**1. Choose the best option for blank 1.**

- A. who appeared
- B. who was appeared
- C. which appeared
- D. whom appeared

答案：who appeared

解析：先行词 a volunteer 指人，在定语从句中作主语，用 who；appear 是不及物动词，没有被动形式，故 who was appeared 错误。which 不能指人；whom 只能作宾语，不能作主语。本题考查定语从句关系代词与语态。

**2. Choose the best option for blank 2.**

- A. built
- B. building
- C. being built
- D. to build

答案：built

解析：the bridge 与 build 是被动关系，且建桥发生在 1980 年、已完成，用过去分词 built 作后置定语，与后面的 carrying 并列。building 表主动进行；being built 表“正在被建”；to build 表将来或目的，均与 in 1980 不符。本题考查过去分词作定语。

**3. Choose the best option for blank 3.**

- A. records
- B. recording
- C. recorded
- D. to record

答案：records

解析：in which 引导的定语从句中，主语是 she，谓语需用一般现在时第三人称单数 records，与主句 keeps 的时态一致。recording 是分词，从句缺谓语；recorded 时态与“每天的小事”不符；to record 不能作谓语。本题考查定语从句中的主谓一致与时态。

**4. Choose the best option for blank 4.**

- A. Having discussed
- B. Discussed
- C. Being discussed
- D. To discuss

答案：Having discussed

解析：主语 she 与 discuss 是主动关系，且讨论发生在 decided 之前，用现在分词完成式 Having discussed 作状语。Discussed 与 Being discussed 都表被动，与 she 主动讨论矛盾；To discuss 表目的或将来，不能表示先发生的动作。本题考查非谓语动词的主动、被动与时间关系。

**5. Choose the best option for blank 5.**

- A. whether the plan would work
- B. that the plan would work
- C. what the plan would work
- D. which the plan would work

答案：whether the plan would work

解析：not sure 后接 whether 引导的宾语从句，表示“不确定计划是否行得通”，与后句“问了很多问题”呼应。that 引导陈述内容，与“不确定”矛盾；what 与 which 在从句中需作成分，而 work 是不及物动词，从句不缺宾语。本题考查 whether 引导的宾语从句。

**6. Choose the best option for blank 6.**

- A. Although
- B. Because
- C. Unless
- D. Since

答案：Although

解析：主句 the family still walked to the park 中的 still 表明前后是让步关系，用 Although。Because 与 Since 表原因，与“仍然去了公园”的转折逻辑冲突；Unless 表否定条件，语义不通。本题考查让步状语从句连词。

**7. Choose the best option for blank 7.**

- A. if he were
- B. if he is
- C. if he had been
- D. if he would be

答案：if he were

解析：父亲说的是与现在事实相反的假设（他现在并不负责），且主句为 he would ask，构成虚拟语气，条件从句用 if he were。if he is 是真实条件句，与 would ask 不匹配；if he had been 与过去事实相反，时态不符；if he would be 不能用于条件从句。本题考查与现在事实相反的虚拟条件句。

**8. Choose the best option for blank 8.**

- A. has been done
- B. have been done
- C. was done
- D. is done

答案：has been done

解析：主语 most of the shopping 是不可数名词，视为单数；Since 引导的时间状语要求现在完成时，购物是被做的，故用 has been done。have been done 主谓不一致；was done 与 Since 冲突；is done 缺少“已经完成”的含义。本题考查主谓一致与现在完成时被动语态。

**9. Choose the best option for blank 9.**

- A. the first train
- B. a first train
- C. first train
- D. the first trains

答案：the first train

解析：序数词前用定冠词 the 表示“第一班”，且 leaves 为单数谓语，主语应为单数，故 the first train。a first train 中不定冠词不能与序数词连用表示“第一班”；first train 缺少冠词；the first trains 与 leaves 主谓不一致。本题考查冠词与主谓一致。

**10. Choose the best option for blank 10.**

- A. formed
- B. forming
- C. to form

## M26-A-READING-01 · reading · 科技

词数约 330

When Westfield High installed forty laptops in its science rooms three years ago, the staff expected marks to rise within a term. They did not. The first year produced almost no change, and two teachers quietly stopped using the machines. What the school learned in the second year was less exciting and more useful: a device only helps when a clear task sits behind it.

The clearest example came from a chemistry class. Instead of letting students search for answers, the teacher gave each group a faulty experiment and asked them to find the mistake with the laptops. Students had to compare three sources, write down where the sources disagreed, and defend their conclusion in front of the class. The work took twice as long as a normal lesson, and the teacher covered fewer topics that term. Yet the same students later scored noticeably higher on questions that required judging between two similar explanations.

A second lesson concerned attention. A group of fifteen-year-olds was asked to keep a simple log of every time they switched tasks on a screen. Most recorded more than sixty switches in one lesson. When the same class was given a printed sheet and told to work without a screen for twenty minutes, the average number of switches fell to four. Nothing about the students had changed; the tool had.

The school now writes two rules into every lesson plan. First, the purpose of the screen must be written in one sentence that a student could repeat. Second, at least one part of the lesson must happen without it. Teachers report that the second rule is harder to follow than the first, because a silent classroom feels less productive than a busy one, even when less is happening.

None of this means computers are harmful. It means that a machine cannot decide why a lesson exists, and the person who can is still standing at the front of the room.

**1. What is the main idea of the passage?**

- A. Screens help learning only when a clear task is placed behind them.
- B. Laptops should replace printed materials in science lessons.
- C. Marks rise as soon as a school buys modern equipment.
- D. Teachers should let students search freely for answers.

答案：A

解析：全文围绕“设备只有在明确任务支持下才有效”展开，首段末句与末段共同点明这一结论。B 项与文中“屏幕只是工具”的立场不符，文章从未主张取代纸质材料；C 项与第一年“几乎没有变化”直接矛盾；D 项恰是文中被否定的做法（unstructured searching）。本题考查主旨概括。

**2. What can be inferred about the chemistry lesson?**

- A. Covering fewer topics can lead to deeper understanding.
- B. Slow lessons always produce better marks than fast ones.
- C. Students learn best when they work without any sources.
- D. Experiments should be replaced by written exercises.

答案：A

解析：文中说这堂课耗时是平时的两倍、覆盖话题更少，但学生后来在“需要判断两种相似解释”的题上得分明显更高，可推出“少覆盖、深理解”。B 项 always 过于绝对；C 项与“比较三个来源”直接矛盾；D 项文中无依据。本题考查基于细节的推理。

**4. In the passage, the word "log" is closest in meaning to ______.**

- B. a fixed rule
- C. a teaching plan

答案：A

解析：keep a simple log of every time they switched tasks 后接“大多数记录了一节课六十多次切换”，说明 log 是“记录”。B 项 rule 与后文“记录次数”无关；C 项 plan 与“统计切换次数”不符；D 项把 log 误解为测量仪器。本题考查词义猜测。

## M26-A-READING-02 · reading · 历史遗产

词数约 335

The wall around the old town of Anhe was built during the Ming period, largely from grey bricks fired in kilns two kilometres upstream. For two centuries it kept out floods and, occasionally, soldiers. By 1990, however, four hundred metres of it had collapsed, and much of the rest was held together in places by cement that trapped rainwater inside the brickwork. The cement, meant to save the wall, was quietly destroying it.

The repair that began in 2012 looked, at first, like a step backwards. Conservators removed the cement by hand, refilled the joints with a lime mixture close to the original recipe, and left large sections of damaged surface uncovered. Visitors complained that the wall looked unfinished. Three summers later, the same visitors were told something they could not see: the movement of the wall had slowed from nine millimetres a year to under two.

Money was the harder problem. The town spends about 1.2 million yuan each year on maintenance, more than it earns from tickets. Most of it comes from a provincial fund that must be applied for annually, and the application requires evidence that the previous year's work actually held. A local architect, Chen Rui, has argued for years that the town should stop treating the wall as a single object and start treating it as a system of drains, paths and slopes that all move together.

The students of the town middle school now spend two days each term measuring cracks with simple gauges and entering the numbers into a shared table. Their teacher says the point is not to turn them into conservators but to make them notice that a wall is never finished. "A monument is not a thing you look at once," she says. "It is a thing somebody has to keep looking after."

**1. What is the main idea of the passage?**

- A. Keeping an old structure alive demands repeated care rather than one grand repair.
- B. A town should replace an old wall with a modern one.
- C. Students should be trained to become professional conservators.
- D. Visitors' complaints decide how public money is spent.

答案：A

解析：全文以城墙修缮为主线，强调最初的水泥“修复”反而有害、后续需要逐年维护与监测，末段“a wall is never finished”点明主旨。B 项文中无据，文章始终主张保护而非替换；C 项与教师“不是要把他们培养成修复师”直接矛盾；D 项把游客意见的作用夸大为决策依据。本题考查主旨概括。

**2. What can be inferred about the removal of the cement?**

- A. Work that looked like damage actually slowed the wall's movement.
- B. The wall moved faster after the cement was removed.
- C. Visitors were right that the repair had failed.
- D. The original bricks were too soft to be reused.

答案：A

解析：文中说去除水泥“起初看起来像倒退”、游客抱怨“没修完”，但三年后城墙位移从每年九毫米降到两毫米以下，说明看似破坏的处理实际起了保护作用。B 项与位移下降相反；C 项与“游客看不到的改善”矛盾；D 项文中未提砖质问题。本题考查基于评价与数据的推理。

**3. Why does the funding application require evidence from the previous year?**

- A. To ensure that public money supports work that actually holds.
- B. To reduce the total amount spent on maintenance each year.
- C. To employ more local architects in the town.
- D. To attract more visitors to the old town.

答案：A

解析：文中说明申请需“证明确实稳住了”，即用效果作为继续拨款的前提，目的是让资金投向真正有效的维护。B 项与“每年仍花 120 万”不构成必然联系；C 项与拨款条件无关；D 项文中未提旅游收益与申请条件的关系。本题考查对制度条件的理解。

**4. As used in the passage, the word "conservators" most nearly means ______.**

- A. people trained to repair and preserve old buildings
- B. local officials who control the town's budget
- C. visitors who study the history of a place
- D. workers who fire bricks in kilns

答案：A

解析：conservators 在文中执行“手工去除水泥、按原配方调配灰浆”等专业操作，指受过训练的文保修复人员。B 项与“拨款申请”这一角色不同；C 项 visitors 在文中有明确区分；D 项 kilns 里的工人是制砖者，不参与修缮。本题考查词义猜测。
