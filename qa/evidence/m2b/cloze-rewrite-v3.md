## 1. She felt (1) ______ before the big exam.

A. anxious
B. joyful
C. calm
D. proud

**答案：** anxious

**context_clue：** She was worried and nervous all morning.

**clue_argument：** The clue 'She was worried and nervous all morning' points to a negative, uneasy feeling, so 'anxious' fits. 'Joyful' is excluded because worry and nervousness are not happy feelings; 'calm' is excluded because it is the opposite of nervous; 'proud' is excluded because pride is not described by worry and nervousness.

**解析：** 线索句“She was worried and nervous all morning”说明她感到担忧和紧张，因此应选 anxious（焦虑的）。joyful 表示开心，与担忧紧张矛盾；calm 意为平静，正好与 nervous 相反；proud 意为自豪，线索中并未体现自豪感。词汇要点：anxious 是描述担忧、紧张情绪的常用形容词。

- joyful: grammar=True semantic=False idiomatic=True — Contradicts the clue that she was worried and nervous.
- calm: grammar=True semantic=False idiomatic=True — Opposite of being worried and nervous.
- proud: grammar=True semantic=False idiomatic=True — Pride is not indicated by worry and nervousness.
